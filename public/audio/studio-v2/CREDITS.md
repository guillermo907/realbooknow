Recordings: **Greg Sullivan**. SFZ mapping and corrections: **kinwie**.
Pinned source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/GregSullivan.E-Pianos/tree/8c3e581acda3594b553948ff0222d4f84a698376
License of this pinned distribution: **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ . Full text: LICENSE-Greg-Sullivan.txt.
Changes by Jazzbook: curated keyboard range, AAC conversion at 192 kbps, duration limits, manifest extraction of key/velocity ranges and tuning, fixed instrument-level gain, native Web Audio playback. ## Black And Blue Basses — darkblack hollowbody
Recordings and mapping: **D. Smolken / Karoryfer Samples**.
Pinned source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-blue-basses/tree/6e7d674cdb41be7a54dbccb15472401ad01099b9
License: **CC0 1.0 Universal** — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Full text: LICENSE-CC0.txt.
Changes by Jazzbook: curated pitches/round robins, AAC conversion at 192 kbps, sounding-pitch mapping, duration limits, fixed instrument gain. Included articulations: ordinary finger plucks (four dynamic layers and four variants), real ghost plucks (two layers/four variants), short/staccato notes and release noises. These are the hollowbody recordings, not the separate picked babyblue instrument. This subset does **not** contain slap or pop recordings. No transcribed bass lines or SFZ engine code is included.

The original recorded upright and drum kit are preserved. Playable basses use recordings; synthesized choices have been retired. This bank is hosted with the static application, loaded only when selected, and needs no paid service or runtime CDN.

## Salamander Yamaha C5 grand
Recordings: **Alexander Holm**, CC-BY-3.0. Pinned source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano/tree/3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404 . Full legal text: LICENSE-Salamander.txt.
Jazzbook adaptations: 14 recorded pitches spanning A2–C6, eight original velocity layers, original retuning corrections, 32 kHz stereo AAC 256 kbps, up to ten seconds with end fade, instrument-wide gain trim. Natural recorded decay is retained without the early sustain loop. This is a curated accompaniment range, not the complete source library.

## VCSL Steinway B grand
Recordings: **Versilian Studios / Sam Gossner**, CC0-1.0. Pinned source: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL/tree/c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e . Full legal text: LICENSE-CC0.txt.
Jazzbook adaptations: close-microphone non-pedal recordings, 21 recorded pitches A#2–D6, all three available touch layers, 32 kHz stereo AAC 256 kbps, up to ten seconds with end fade, instrument-wide gain trim. Natural recorded decay is retained. Neither grand is an EQ variation of Splendid.

## Growlybass — Squier Jazz Bass
Recordings: **D. Smolken / Karoryfer Samples**, CC0-1.0. Pinned source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.growlybass/tree/4f483268fc66b5a6d5781d421c0d11b8d08d3fc6 . Full legal text: LICENSE-CC0.txt.
Real Squier Jazz Bass, fingerstyle DI, roundwounds and both pickups. Four dynamic layers/four alternate plucks; separate staccato and release recordings (five variants). Jazzbook adaptations: sounding-octave correction, curated E1–G3 coverage, 32 kHz AAC 256 kbps, length limits/end fades and one instrument-wide gain trim. No slap/pop samples. Ghosts reuse the separately recorded pitchless Black hollowbody dead plucks.

## Black And Blue Basses — babyblue picked solidbody
Recordings: **D. Smolken / Karoryfer Samples**, CC0-1.0. Same pinned repository and license as darkblack above. Two dynamic layers and six alternate picked plucks, curated E1–G3 range. Jazzbook adaptations: sounding-octave mapping, AAC 256 kbps/32 kHz, up to 5.5 seconds, end fades and instrument-wide trim. Ghosts reuse darkblack dead plucks. This instrument is not called a Precision Bass.

## Old Fender P-Bass Picked
Recordings: **tim.kahn (formerly Corsica_S)**, CC-BY-4.0. Pack: https://freesound.org/people/tim.kahn/packs/9130/ . Full legal text: LICENSE-CC-BY-4.txt.
Individual sound pages: https://freesound.org/people/tim.kahn/sounds/148247/ through https://freesound.org/people/tim.kahn/sounds/148258/ inclusive. Each page identifies a real Fender P-Bass played with a pick and licenses the recording under CC-BY-4.0.
Public HQ MP3 renditions are bundled unchanged; per-note gain and pitch mapping are external manifest metadata. One touch per pitch, no round robins, slap or pop; the author describes old strings and string buzz. This is honestly labelled a vintage picked recording, not claimed equivalent to a modern multi-articulation commercial instrument. Shared muted ghost recordings come from darkblack above. Original WAV downloads require a Freesound login; no account was used.

All recordings are redistributed under their stated licenses with attribution and modifications identified here. No upstream SFZ engine code, arrangement patterns or copyleft implementation code is included.
